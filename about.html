About us

PizzaShop started as a single kitchen with one goal: get honest, fresh pizza to your door in 30 minutes, or it's free. Here's the full story of how we got here, and where we're headed next.

Our Story

In just a couple of years, we have opened 6 outlets in different cities: Lviv, Kyiv, Odesa, Chernivtsi, Kharkiv, and in the future we plan to develop the network in other major cities of Ukraine.

Our Kitchens

The kitchen of each point is at least 400-500 sq. m., with hundreds of employees smoothly performing work in order to receive, prepare, form and deliver customer orders on time.

Our Mission

We believe great pizza shouldn't mean a long wait. Every order is made from scratch with hand-stretched dough and delivered fast, without cutting corners on quality.

Where To Find Us

You'll find a PizzaShop kitchen in Lviv, Kyiv, Odesa, Chernivtsi and Kharkiv today, with new locations opening as we grow across Ukraine.
