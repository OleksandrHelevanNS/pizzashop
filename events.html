Events

There are regular events in our pizzeria that let you enjoy delicious food for a lower price. Here's the full rundown of what's on right now, and the ongoing perks you can count on every time you visit.

How We're Cooking

Watch our chefs hand-stretch dough and build every pizza from scratch. Drop by during open kitchen hours to see the full process before your order hits the oven.

Our Blog

Recipes, ingredient stories and behind-the-scenes updates from our kitchens, posted regularly so you never miss a new topping combination or seasonal special.

Two Pizzas for One Price

Order any two pizzas from the menu and pay for one. Available every Tuesday and Wednesday, dine-in or delivery.

Kitchen Tour

Book a free guided tour of one of our 400-500 sq. m. kitchens and see exactly how your pizza gets from dough to doorstep.

Free Coffee for 3 Pizzas

Buy three pizzas in a single order and we'll add a free coffee for the table, on us.

Our Instagram

Follow us for early looks at limited-time pizzas, event announcements and giveaways posted throughout the week.

Where Are You Located?

We currently operate six outlets across Lviv, Kyiv, Odesa, Chernivtsi and Kharkiv, with more cities planned as the network grows.
